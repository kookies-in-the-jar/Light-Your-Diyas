The Science of Lighting a Clay Diya

In Vedic traditions, the clay lamp represents the human body, the sesame or mustard oil symbolizes human tendencies (gunas), and the cotton wick portrays the self (atma). By lighting the wick with the flame of divine knowledge, inner negativity is dispelled and enlightened bliss spreads to all around.
